# Hotel artwork

The portraits were made with image generation using the corresponding concierge, scholar and messenger illustrations as character references.

- `portrait-moss.png`: sage concierge, orange hat, brass key and luggage.
- `portrait-inkwell.png`: blue scholar, spectacles, scarf and book.
- `portrait-pip.png`: rose messenger, curled horns, envelope and travel bag.

Prompt direction: preserve character identity and accessories; square white gallery background; thousands of short ink strokes, crosshatched colored marks, stippled dots, luminous dashes and woven pen texture. Restrained sage, blue, rose, ochre and black. Strong silhouette, generous margins, no text or interface.

These are generated still images, not executable procedural drawings. Profiles reuse three character families; moving characters are Three.js voxel geometry.
