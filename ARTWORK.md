# Hotel artwork

Each of the 41 named residents has a separately generated ink portrait and an individual voxel model in `characters.js`. Portraits are still illustrations, not executable procedural drawings. Player-created rooms can choose an existing portrait family; they are separate from the named hotel cast.

## Art direction

Original characters on white, with engraved pen strokes, crosshatching, stippled detail and restrained color. Each design has its own silhouette, anatomy, material and hotel accessory. No recolored copies. Images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on September 15, 2026, then copied into `assets/`.
